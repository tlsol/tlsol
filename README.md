Founder of **[compute.inc](https://compute.inc)**. I build and run Internet infrastructure: our own backbone, our own IP space, and hardware in **34 datacentres across 23 cities**, from Auckland to Toronto.

I put my first server online in 2017, when I was eleven. Since then I've sold two companies, taken a third past $2M ARR, and grown the compute.inc group past $60M a year. Based between Oxford, Kent and Hong Kong.
